Percentages compound, and each result is rounded up to the nearest 10 before the next one is applied. War +20 then Tariff +30 gives ×1.56, not ×1.50 — and 25mm pipe goes 60 → 80 → 110, which is why individual rows move by more than 56%. This is the model the BOM and the printed team guide books are built on. Manual adjustments land last, on top of whatever the events have already done.

Buyback never moves. It is unused / cut-but-usable against the base price, whatever the board says at the time. Unusable material is bought back at nothing.
