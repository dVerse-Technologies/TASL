# Award Categories

**Not competitive.** The theme is subsystems working together for the organisation's success, so awards recognise qualities rather than rank teams.

## Structure of the wrap-up

Open with the **collective result** before any award — both balls, and the gap between them in seconds. Not a prize, just the number. It frames everything after it as recognition rather than ranking, and makes the point that the room succeeded or didn't as one system.

Then **one award per team**, chosen from the pool below. Ten teams of six senior people is small enough that a team receiving nothing will notice, and that undercuts the theme.

Fourteen candidates so the crew can match honestly rather than force a fit.

## The pool

| Award | Recognises | How to spot it |
|---|---|---|
| **Good Neighbour** | The handover, judged by the teams either side | Nominated by adjacent teams only — the one award decided by peers |
| **The Long View** | Changed their own design so someone else's job became possible | Rebuilt something that already worked, for another team's benefit |
| **Frugal Engineer** | Most result per Kasu | Working module, money still in the tin |
| **Best Trade** | One negotiation that left both sides better off | Trade log; ask both parties separately |
| **Market Maker** | Kept material moving whether or not it profited them | Named by several unrelated teams as someone they dealt with |
| **Elegant Solution** | The simplest mechanism that did the job | Fewest parts, least fuss, obviously right once you see it |
| **Improvisation** | Solved it with material never intended for that use | Cardboard doing a job PVC was meant to do |
| **Measure Twice** | Numbers right the first time | Measured height matched claimed height |
| **The Clean Build** | Assembly quality | Square cuts, no wobble, nothing held together by hope |
| **Weathered the Storm** | Best response to the war and tariff | Bought early, or adapted rather than complained |
| **Called It Early** | Anticipated something before it happened | Stockpiled or repositioned ahead of a shift |
| **The Debug** | Found and fixed a fault fast — theirs or someone else's | Usually the team that walked over to help |
| **Held the Line** | Recovered from a setback without derailing the room | Something collapsed and they rebuilt without drama |
| **Best Looking Run** | The module people wanted to photograph | Self-evident |

## Protect Good Neighbour

It's the only award teams decide about each other, and it's closest to the actual lesson — your subsystem is judged by the people it hands to, not by how good it looks on its own. Consider giving it twice.

## This depends on notes

These have to be decided from what someone actually noticed at 1:45, not invented during the wrap-up. Whoever is on the floor keeps running notes on their teams throughout.
