# Decisions Log

## Economy

**Coins are uniform.** Every team gets 2,000 Kasu in identical denominations (500×2, 100×3, 50×8, 20×10, 10×10). All asymmetry lives in the materials. Uniform cash means no team can attribute its outcome to its endowment, which keeps the debrief clean.

**Buyback stays 100% fresh / 50% modified**, for speed at the counter during a rush. Buyback is valued against the **base price**, never the live price — otherwise a team that stockpiles before the war announcement can sell back at the higher price for risk-free profit.

**All prices are multiples of 20**, so the 50% buyback always lands on an amount payable with 10/20/50/100/500 coins.

**MOQ is 1** on every depot line.

## Materials

**Depot stock = 1.5 × basekit quantity** on every line. 100% for trading, 50% as reserve. Designed scarcity comes from unequal kits and from price — never from an empty shelf. A stockout makes the price mechanism meaningless and forces the crew to improvise rulings.

**Kits supply about 85% of each team's structural frame**, and structural prices were halved. At the earlier 30% level, closing the structural gap cost the room 32,360 Kasu against 20,000 in circulation — teams would have run out of money before their frames were standing. Frames are the boring part and should not eat the budget.

**Balls are not sold at the depot.** Ball size is a God Mode lever.

**Removed:** telescopic slip coupler, inspection bend with access door, hose clamps.

**Added to kits:** permanent marker (1 each), rubber mallet (1 each), A3 sheets (2 each), 32mm tee, 32mm end cap, 76mm split bend, 2 narrow buckets for Team 10.

**Added to depot only:** funnel, priced at 200 so building a cardboard catcher stays the cheaper option — Teams 4 and 5 still have to solve their own catch.

## Sizing

**Standardised to mm.** All inch notation dropped; mm is what's printed on the pipe.

- Structural supports — **25mm OD, 15kg**
- Closed pathway — **32mm OD, 10kg** (ID ≈ 28mm)
- Open pathway — **76mm split lengthways, SWR Type B**

Three-way and four-way corners are **PVC furniture fittings**, a different product line from plumbing stock and sourced separately. A plumbing "cross" is flat and cannot build a box frame.

## Room information design

**No inventory board and no want/have board.** Both need maintenance nobody has time for, and both go stale within the hour. Replaced by two things that maintain themselves:

- **Large table numbers**, readable across the hall
- **A spares tray on every table** — teams put what they don't need where others can see it. It cannot go stale, because it *is* the inventory.

## Depot operation

**Nandha and Yashwanth are paired on the depot** — Yashwanth on the transaction counter, Nandha on a separate cutting bay with power tools. Navaneeth works both queues.

**Participants never touch power tools.** No exceptions.

**Cutting bay: free, max 5 cuts per team per visit**, then back of the queue. Teams have hand tools, so the bay is a speed upgrade rather than a necessity — the queue is the price. Without a cap, one person with a saw becomes the bottleneck for the whole room.
