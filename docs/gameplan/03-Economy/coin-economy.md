# Coin Economy

Currency: **Kasu**. Denominations 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 / 500.

| 20 | 10 | 200 |
| 10 | 10 | 100 |
| **Total** | **33 coins** | **2,000** |

Circulation across ten teams: **20,000 Kasu, 330 coins**.

## Depot floating stash — 440 coins, 10,000 Kasu

| 10 | 20 | 50 | 100 | 500 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 150 | 80 | 10 | 0 |

**No 500s at the counter.** The depot only ever receives them. Its job is handing back small coins, so the float is weighted small.

## Depot reserve stash — 470 coins

Used to replace damaged or lost coins, and to fund God Mode injections.

## Why circulation is 20,000

Room-wide purchasing at base prices comes to roughly 16,000 Kasu — about 80% of circulation. Tight enough that the war and tariff bite, loose enough that nobody is stranded.

Two properties worth knowing on the day:

**The 500 coin barely moves before the shocks.** Most base prices are under 500 and teams pay exact with smaller coins. After war and tariff, several items cross 500 and the big coins start circulating — the denomination becomes useful exactly when the squeeze arrives.

**Money drains out of the room over the afternoon.** Teams are net buyers, so coins accumulate at the depot and circulation in team hands falls. That tightens the squeeze on its own, on top of the price rises. Don't over-correct with a bailout at 2:30 — the room getting poorer is the design working.
