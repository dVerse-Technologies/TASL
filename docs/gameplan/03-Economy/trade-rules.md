# Trade Rules

1. The depot sells at the **live** price on the board. The board is the truth.
2. The depot buys back at the **base** price, whatever the board says at the time.
3. Fresh and unused → 100% of base. Cut or modified but still usable → 50% of base. Unusable → rejected, no value.
4. The depot transacts in board units only.
5. **One nominated Trader per team.** Only Traders approach the counter — sixty people at one counter stops the game.
6. Team-to-team trade is unrestricted: material, coins, labour, tools on loan, favours.
7. Safety gear is **not tradeable**. It stays with the team.
8. **Cutting bay:** free, maximum 5 cuts per team per visit, then back of the queue. Participants never touch the power tools.

## Why buyback is against base price

With buyback at the live price, a team that stockpiles before the war announcement sells back afterwards at 20% more — risk-free profit that drains the coin stash and inflates the money supply at exactly the moment you were trying to squeeze it.

Print two columns on the board: **live buy price** (changes) and **base value** (fixed). Depot staff read a fixed printed number and halve it. Same mental load, no arbitrage.
