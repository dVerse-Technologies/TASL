## Ball upgrade — the rules that make it work

**Per path, not per team.** Ball size is a system-wide parameter for everything downstream of the launcher. The two paths are independent until Team 10, so per-path is the correct granularity.

**Locked at 2:00.** From 2:00 to 3:00 Teams 8 and 9 are calibrating arrival times. A bigger ball has a different loss profile, so handing one over at 2:40 voids every measurement they have taken.

**Frame it as a spec change, not a bailout.** Announcing *"the client has revised the payload spec"* removes the stigma, avoids teams learning that visible struggle produces free help, and turns a mercy into the most realistic lesson of the day.

**It helps everyone except Team 8**, whose job is to brake the fast path. Expect their demand for flexi hose and foam to spike the moment you announce.
