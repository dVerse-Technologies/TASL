# Sizing Standard

All sizes are **pipe OD in mm** — what's printed on the pipe. **kg** denotes pressure class, which is wall thickness. Inch notation is not used anywhere.

| Use | Spec |
|---|---|
| Structural supports and frames | **25mm OD, 15kg** |
| Closed pathway (ball travels inside) | **32mm OD, 10kg** — ID ≈ 28mm |
| Open pathway | **76mm split lengthways, SWR Type B** |

## Why these

**25mm at 15kg** for structure — the thicker wall resists flexing, which matters because a springy frame absorbs the momentum Team 2 needs for the climb. It's also the size PVC furniture fittings are actually available in.

**32mm at 10kg** for pathway — ID ≈ 28mm passes a 25mm ball with 1.5mm clearance per side. Verified physically.

**76mm SWR Type B** for the open channel — Type A is thinner and a split length will sag and flatten. A split pipe loses nearly all its hoop stiffness.

## Sourcing notes

**Three-way and four-way corners are PVC furniture fittings**, a different product line from plumbing stock. A plumbing "cross" is flat — all four outlets in one plane — and cannot build a box frame. Mostly available in 20mm and 25mm only, and not stocked by ordinary plumbing counters.

**Long-radius 90° bends and Y junctions** are common in SWR and uncommon in 32mm pressure fittings. Confirm availability before finalising.

**76mm cannot be cut with kit tools.** A PVC scissor cutter tops out around 42mm. All 76mm is pre-split and pre-cut before the day.

## Frame geometry

| Structure | 25mm pipe | Three-way | Four-way |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1m cube, 1 level | 12 m | 8 | 0 |
| 1m cube, 2 levels | 20 m | 8 | 4 |

Two stacked cubes: 12 horizontal edges plus 8 vertical. Bottom and top corners are three-way; the four mid-height corners take a vertical from above and below, so they're four-way.
